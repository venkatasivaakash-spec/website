Statutory Acts & Regulatory Compliance

Key Indian laws and regulatory frameworks relevant to businesses, professionals, employers and individuals. Our team can help assess applicability and support compliance.

Direct Taxation

  • Income-tax Act, 2025 and Income-tax Rules, 2026
  • Income-tax Act, 1961 and Rules for legacy tax years and proceedings
  • Finance Acts, tax deduction and collection at source (TDS/TCS)
  • Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA)
  • Transfer pricing and international taxation provisions

GST & Indirect Taxes

  • Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017
  • Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017
  • State Goods and Services Tax Acts
  • Union Territory GST Act and GST Compensation to States Act
  • Customs Act, 1962 and Customs Tariff Act, 1975, where applicable
  • Professional Tax laws of the relevant State

Company & Entity Law

  • Companies Act, 2013 and applicable Rules
  • Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008
  • Indian Partnership Act, 1932
  • Societies Registration laws and Trust laws, where applicable
  • Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
  • Competition Act, 2002, where applicable

Audit & Financial Reporting

  • Companies Act audit and reporting requirements
  • Cost and Works Accountants Act, 1959, where applicable
  • Accounting Standards / Ind AS notified under applicable law
  • Standards on Auditing and ICAI guidance
  • CARO and internal financial controls reporting, where applicable

Business, Banking & Foreign Exchange

  • Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA)
  • Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and applicable RBI directions
  • Banking Regulation Act, 1949, where applicable
  • Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA)
  • Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988
  • Black Money Act, 2015, where applicable

Employment & Payroll

  • Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952
  • Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948
  • Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
  • Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
  • Minimum Wages Act, 1948 and applicable wage laws
  • Payment of Wages Act, 1936
  • Maternity Benefit Act, 1961
  • State Shops and Establishments and Professional Tax laws

Commercial & Consumer Laws

  • Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Sale of Goods Act, 1930
  • Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019
  • Legal Metrology Act, 2009, where applicable
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable rules

Property & Transactions

  • Transfer of Property Act, 1882
  • Registration Act, 1908
  • Indian Stamp Act, 1899 and State stamp laws
  • Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)
  • Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013, where relevant

Sector-Specific & Other Laws

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules/commencement provisions, as applicable
  • Environment protection and pollution-control laws, where relevant
  • Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSAI), for food businesses
  • Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, for regulated products
  • Electricity Act, 2003 and sectoral rules, where relevant
  • State-specific licences, registrations and local-body laws
